All notable changes to **Millar** (Hahlex Design System) are documented here following [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-25 (Draft Foundation Release)

### Added
- **Governance & Architecture**: Accepted ADRs 0001-0005 locking W3C DTCG `2025.10` SSoT, unified `Geist` + `Inter` + `Geist Mono` typography, unified OKLCH Warm Sand/Bronze/Espresso palette with register-scoped gradient rules, standalone monorepo (`d:\projects\millar`), mandatory CMS origin isolation before `hahlex.com/millar` production launch, MCP `2026-07-28` contract (Resources + 11 Tools + 3 Prompts), and 6 diverse Marketing Composition Patterns.
- **Visual Philosophy**: Authored *Measured Warmth* aesthetic philosophy manifesto (`docs/art-direction/measured-warmth-philosophy.md`).
- **Governance Tooling**: Added `check:em-dash` (Hahlex-controlled UI/docs scope) and `check:governance` (Draft/Approved/Deprecated sign-off gate).
