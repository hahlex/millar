# World-Class Web Design Aesthetic Movements

Consult this taxonomy during Step 1 when establishing a new visual world or translating a user's vague taste words ("sang", "tinh tế", "hiện đại", "editorial", "warm", "technical") into concrete structural rules. Always verify compatibility with the user's brief and Hahlex invariants.

## 1. Measured Warmth (Hahlex Canonical Standard)
- **Lineage:** Dieter Rams precision + Nordic paper materiality + warm editorial print craft.
- **Palette DNA:** Warm Cream / Warm Sand (`#FAF8F5`, `#FAF3EA`, `#F2E6CD`) alternating with Deep Espresso (`#1A130D`, `#120C08`), grounded by CTA Brown (`#4A3022`) and disciplined Hahlex Crimson (`#B3121B` light / `#F0857F` dark, `< 1.5%` tension budget).
- **Type & Grid:** Geist Sans (light 300-400 monumental display, 600 operational headers) + Inter body + Geist Mono tabular data. Visible structural restraint, generous whitespace, zero stock-photo crutches.
- **Best for:** Hahlex ecosystem, education platforms, thoughtful productivity software, human-centered AI tools.

## 2. Swiss International / Architectural Grid
- **Lineage:** Josef Muller-Brockmann, Vignelli, modern engineering documentation (Vercel Geist, Stripe Docs).
- **Palette DNA:** Crisp high-contrast neutral ground (`#FFFFFF` or `#0A0A0A`), razor-thin 1px coordinate rules, surgical single chromatic accent.
- **Type & Grid:** Strict mathematical grid, flush-left asymmetric typography, exposed structural hairlines, tabular indices, generous active negative space.
- **Best for:** Developer platforms, design system portals, technical specifications, API documentation.

## 3. Editorial Minimalism (Curatorial Print-on-Web)
- **Lineage:** Kinfolk, Cereal, Are.na, Anthropic editorial essays, architectural monographs.
- **Palette DNA:** Unbleached archival paper tones, charcoal ink (never harsh pure `#000000`), quiet warm sepia/stone secondary borders.
- **Type & Grid:** Dramatic display-to-body scale contrast, `65ch` column discipline, full-bleed or carefully matted art plates, asymmetrical text-and-figure splits, pull-quotes anchored by thin vertical rules.
- **Best for:** Brand manifestos, case studies, cultural/research institutions, high-trust storytelling.

## 4. Precision Dark Engineering (Technical Craft)
- **Lineage:** Linear, Raycast, Cron, high-precision audio/optical instruments.
- **Palette DNA:** Deep carbon/obsidian layers with subtle tonal steps (`#0B0C0E` -> `#14161A` -> `#1C1F26`), crisp low-opacity hairline borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), restrained specular light edges, disciplined status hues.
- **Type & Grid:** Tight tracking on headings, keyboard-first affordances (`Cmd+K`, keycaps), live interactive telemetry and real product chrome front-and-center.
- **Best for:** Pro developer tools, observability dashboards, power-user workspaces.

## 5. Tactile Craft & Organic Materiality
- **Lineage:** Wabi-sabi craft, bookbinding, ceramics, physical stationery brought into digital interfaces.
- **Palette DNA:** Earth pigments, mineral neutrals, subtle micro-grain or matte paper contrast, soft directional natural shadows.
- **Type & Grid:** Human-scaled proportions, gentle rounded radii, tactile pressed states (`scale(0.98)`), deliberate slow-ease entrances with grounded mass.
- **Best for:** Creative studios, wellness, culinary, craft commerce, boutique learning brands.

## 6. Kinetic Typographic Poster (High-Impact Narrative)
- **Lineage:** Experimental poster design, modern launch microsites, Stripe Press / Apple keynote pacing.
- **Palette DNA:** Bold high-contrast blocks (alternating solid monumental bands), zero clutter, typography acting as the primary architectural graphic.
- **Type & Grid:** Oversized display statements (`clamp(2.5rem, 6vw, 5rem)`), pinned numeric proof strips, one choreographed scroll or view-transition centerpiece.
- **Best for:** Flagship launches, annual reports, announcements, conference microsites.

## Translating Vague User Intent to Concrete Design Dials

| User says... | Avoid this trap | Commit to this system |
|---|---|---|
| "Làm cho sang / cao cấp" | Generic gold gradient + black background + fake luxury serif | Generous negative space, high typographic scale contrast, crisp 1px hairlines, restrained palette (`<= 2` accents), matted imagery |
| "Hiện đại / gọn gàng" | Sterile gray cards + generic Tailwind shadows | Architectural spacing rhythm (8pt grid), clear content-first hierarchy, themed browser surfaces, purposeful micro-interactions |
| "Ấm áp / thân thiện" | Pastel rainbow blobs + bubbly cartoon icons | Warm mineral/sand paper surfaces, natural brown/charcoal ink, 1.5px consistent icons, gentle spring feedback |
| "Đậm chất công nghệ / AI" | Purple-pink neon glow + floating particles + fake chatbots | Live interactive product chrome, clean schematic diagrams, tabular numerals, keyboard-first shortcuts, zero decorative glow |
| "Đồng bộ với ảnh/animation tôi đưa" | Copying only font + primary hex onto plain secondary pages | Run `millar-deep-research` extraction -> lock `.millar/style-dna.md` -> propagate lighting, framing, surface cadence, and motion curves to every route |
