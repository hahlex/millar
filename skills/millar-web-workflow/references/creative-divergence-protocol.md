# Creative Divergence & Anti-Slop Calibration Protocol

AI models converge toward a handful of predictable templates unless steered intentionally. Run this protocol during Track L (and on open visual axes in Track M) before locking `.millar/style-dna.md`.

## 1. Brand-First Gate: Research Before Diverging

- **If the user named a brand:** Research its real palette, surface polarity, typography, and visual voice first (local `DESIGN.md`/`PRODUCT.md`, live Brand MCP such as Millar MCP for Hahlex, or web search). Apply creativity to *composition, pacing, storytelling, and interactive craft* inside that verified brand world rather than replacing its identity or hardcoding another brand's tokens.
- **If the brief is greenfield / unbranded:** Run Steps 2-5 below to invent an authentic world from scratch.

## 2. Name and Ban All Four AI Ruts (`Impeccable` Calibration Distilled)

Reject ALL four statistical defaults on open briefs (each is legitimate ONLY when explicitly pinned by the user or researched brand):
1. **Dark-Slate Neon SaaS Rut:** Near-black slate + floating purple/cyan radial glow + centered white H1 with uppercase eyebrow pill + 3 equal glass icon-cards + fake vanity stats.
2. **Warm-Cream Editorial AI Rut:** Warm cream ground + high-contrast serif display + terracotta or signal-red accent applied blindly to every bookish, warm, or human subject (even when that subject's real world is vibrant, industrial, or photographic!).
3. **Broadsheet Costume Rut:** Generic hairline boxes + italic display serif + decorative tracked uppercase mono labels mimicking a newspaper without real editorial substance.
4. **Flat Brutalist Costume Rut:** Saturated ink + heavy condensed type + hard zero-blur `4px 4px 0` black shadows used as decoration without genuine structural intent.

**Training-Data Overused Display Fonts (Avoid on Open Briefs):** `Fraunces`, `Playfair Display`, `Cormorant`, `Lora`, `Crimson`, `Newsreader`, `Syne`, `Space Grotesk`, `Space Mono`, `IBM Plex`, `Inter`-as-display, `DM Sans`, `DM Serif`, `Outfit`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Instrument Sans`. Use one ONLY when a researched brand pins it or a concrete functional requirement (such as full Vietnamese glyph clarity in a UI workhorse face) earns it.

## 3. Choose Color Strategy & Polarity From the Physical Scene

- **Pick Color Strategy Before Picking Hexes:**
  - *Restrained:* Palette-tinted neutrals + 1 disciplined accent (ideal for `Operate` / `Read` or research-pinned quiet editorial brands).
  - *Committed:* One saturated surface color owns `30%-60%` of the page.
  - *Full Palette:* 3-4 intentional structural color roles at page scale.
  - *Drenched:* The color field IS the page ground.
- **Derive Light vs. Dark From Physical Reality:** Write one sentence naming *who uses this surface, where they sit, and under what ambient lighting*, and let that physical scene determine light vs. dark polarity, never category habit.
- **Three-Family Cultural Derivation:** List concrete references spanning at least **3 material/graphic families** from the audience's real world (print traditions, physical architecture/materials, real-world instruments/rituals) rather than copying competitor websites.

## 4. Dual-Soul Art Direction (`canvas-design` + `Impeccable` Essence)

Fuse BOTH qualities across every hero and editorial visual (keeping all art prompts in crisp English):
- **Soul A (1-Second Human Warmth & Chiaroscuro Depth):** Tell the story in 1 second via authentic human scenes, candid daylight photography (`human-editorial-photography.md`), or illuminated architectural thresholds with 3 depth planes.
- **Soul B (Systematic Museum Craft):** When producing code-native plates (`procedural-gallery-craft.md`), reward close zoom with dense engraving craft (18-32 strand parametric curves, halftone topographies, fine hatching, tactile paper/surface grain, `90%` visual form / `10%` typography) and a strict **Zero-Addition Pass 2 polish**.
- **Balanced Polarity Rhythm:** Honor the researched brand's light/dark balance across multi-asset series (never defaulting to 4/4 monochrome dark on a light-canvas brand).

## 5. Author One Unforgettable Signature Moment per Surface

Every `Persuade` or `Experience` page earns **one** dominant authored centerpiece (never 10 competing gimmicks):
- **Live Interactive Specimen:** A real, working micro-version of the product right in the viewport (live filterable audit view, interactive calculator, real component sandbox).
- **Decisive Documentary Visual:** One honest, film-grade human photograph or museum-caliber procedural plate carrying real visual authority.
- **Tactile Spatial Continuity:** Shared-element View Transition or calibrated spatial scroll rhythm where surfaces hold physical mass.
- **Curated Material Contrast:** Deliberate polarity shift across sections obeying the researched brand's surface proportion budget.

On `Operate` and `Read` pages, spend craft on **compounding precision**: instant `Cmd+K` search, snappy keyboard navigation, `tabular-nums`, themed browser surfaces (`::selection`, focus rings, scrollbars), and calm zero-clutter empty states.

## 6. Three-Question Creative Self-Challenge

Before finalizing `.millar/style-dna.md`:
1. *If I hide the logo, would Page 3 still be unmistakably recognizable as the same brand and craft caliber as Page 1?*
2. *Could someone guess my aesthetic purely from the product category (or from "avoid generic SaaS")?* (If yes -> dig deeper.)
3. *Are any two adjacent sections repeating the same card grid, or is any eyebrow chip / decorative gradient / fake metric cluttering truth?* (If yes -> cut it.)
