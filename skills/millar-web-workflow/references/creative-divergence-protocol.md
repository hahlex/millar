# Creative Divergence & Anti-Slop Protocol

AI models converge toward the statistical average of SaaS templates unless explicitly steered away from it. Run this protocol during Track L (and for any open visual dimensions in Track M) before locking `.millar/style-dna.md`.

## 1. Name and Ban "The Rut"

Before sketching layouts, write down the exact cliché this category ships by default, and refuse to build it:
- **Generic AI SaaS Rut:** Dark slate background + floating purple/cyan radial glow + centered white H1 with uppercase eyebrow badge + 3 equal icon-title-paragraph glass cards + fabricated "99.99% uptime / 10k+ teams" vanity strip.
- **Generic Corporate / Edu Rut:** Bland white page + stock photo of smiling people pointing at laptops + blue rounded buttons + identical alternating 50/50 boxes.
- **Generic Secondary-Page Rut:** Rich homepage followed by bare gray-bordered boilerplate tables/cards on `/features`, `/pricing`, and `/about`.

## 2. Three-Family Cultural Derivation (When Creating a New Visual World)

Derive visual language from concrete real-world systems the audience actually trusts, spanning **at least 3 material families** (never stopping at obvious software clichés):
1. **Print & Archival Traditions:** Technical field manuals, architectural monographs, exhibition catalogues, letterpress specimen plates, Swiss railway timetables.
2. **Physical Materials & Environments:** Unbleached cotton rag paper, travertine limestone, brushed anodized aluminum, oiled walnut, drafting vellum, gallery daylight.
3. **Domain Instruments & Rituals:** Precision audio dials, cartographic coordinate ticks, library index cards, botanical plates, laboratory ledgers.

Fuse the strongest structural discipline (grid tension, tonal restraint, framing margin, numeric cadence) with the product's actual functionality. **Honor pinned briefs first:** if the user already pinned a palette, brand system (such as Hahlex *Measured Warmth*), or hero assets, apply creativity to *composition, framing, pacing, and signature interaction* within that pinned world rather than replacing their identity.

## 3. Author One Unforgettable Signature Moment per Surface

Every `Persuade` or `Experience` page deserves **one** genuinely memorable authored centerpiece (never 10 competing gimmicks):
- **Live Interactive Specimen:** A real, playable micro-version of the product right inside the hero or showcase (e.g., live filterable audit table, real theme/contrast lab, interactive architecture schematic) instead of static screenshots.
- **Editorial Typographic Tension:** Monumental light-weight display statement paired with hairline coordinate rules, tabular proof metrics, and asymmetric negative space.
- **Tactile Spatial Continuity:** Shared-element View Transition or calibrated horizontal/stepped canvas flow where elements carry physical mass.
- **Curated Material Contrast:** Deliberate shift from calm sunlit archival paper into one grounded deep-espresso or obsidian showcase band, punctuated by `< 1.5%` high-tension accent detail.

On `Operate` and `Read` pages, channel creativity into **compounding micro-craft**: effortless `Cmd+K` navigation, zero-latency filter pills, crisp tabular alignment, themed browser selection/focus rings, and calm zero-clutter empty states.

## 4. Creative Self-Challenge Check (3 Questions)

Before finalizing `.millar/style-dna.md`, test the proposal honestly:
1. *If I hide the logo, could a viewer still recognize this brand's visual voice on Page 3?* (If no -> raise secondary-page materiality and framing.)
2. *Did any two adjacent sections repeat the exact same card grid or 50/50 split?* (If yes -> rotate composition grammar.)
3. *Is there any decorative gradient, fake metric, or eyebrow badge adding noise instead of truth?* (If yes -> cut it and spend the space on breathing room or real product proof.)
