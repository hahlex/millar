# Natural Editorial Illustration & Organic Linework Playbook (v0.5)

Consult this playbook whenever producing **editorial illustrations, narrative drawings, educational/classroom artwork, hand-drawn spot plates, or organic illustrated web hero scenes** (via AI image generation OR hybrid ink-and-wash craft).

**Core Goal:** Produce artwork that feels genuinely drawn by a master human editorial illustrator (New Yorker, Kinfolk, Monocle, Studio Ghibli sunlit sketchbook, warm Japanese ink-and-watercolor), NEVER stiff mechanical CAD, flat corporate Memphis cartoons, glossy 3D plastic renders, or dark gloomy drama.

---

## 1. The 5 Laws of Natural Drawing & Linework

1. **Variable Hand-Held Line Weight (NEVER Uniform Digital Outlines):**
   - Name real tactile drawing tools: *warm sepia fountain-pen linework with gentle thick-to-thin nib pressure*, *soft 2B graphite pencil contour lines*, *slightly dry Japanese brush-pen ink*, or *loose architectural ink-and-wash*.
   - Allow natural slight taper, expressive micro-imperfection, and occasional broken contour lines where sunlight hits an edge (light dissolves outlines!).
   - **BANNED:** Uniform `1px` mathematical vector outlines, rigid CAD geometry, flat corporate Memphis blob people, heavy black comic outlines.
2. **Translucent Wash & Breathing Paper Reserve:**
   - Layer *translucent watercolor washes, dry-brush matte gouache, or soft colored-pencil shading* with subtle loose register (pigment softly feathers slightly inside or past the ink line instead of filling edges like a paint-bucket tool).
   - Leave **30-45% of raw warm paper tooth visible** as sunlit breathing room and negative space.
3. **Kinetic Mid-Action Storytelling (Specific Concrete Verbs!):**
   - Every human or narrative drawing MUST capture a **specific physical action mid-motion** with real everyday props:
     - *Good:* A teacher kneeling beside a wooden desk sketching a small triangle with a pencil in a student's open margin while the student leans in with chin propped on one hand, eyes brightening mid-realization; ruler, pencil shavings, open textbook, and classmates chatting quietly by sunlit windows behind them.
     - *Bad (REJECT):* Two stiff figures standing beside a desk, one pointing vaguely at a blank page.
4. **Bright, Airy Natural Daylight Default (NO Artificial Gloom!):**
   - Education, learning, mentorship, community, and everyday craft scenes **default to bright sunlit morning or warm afternoon daylight** with open, breathable shadows tinted warm ochre, soft slate, or pale sepia (NEVER heavy black shadows or dark dungeon backgrounds!).
   - Reserve dark night/chiaroscuro illustrations strictly for briefs explicitly set at night, evening, or on dark theatrical themes.
5. **Pigment Language Only (NO Raw `#HEX` Codes Inside Art Prompts!):**
   - Never inject `#1A130D` or `#FFFCF8` hex strings inside illustration prompts; raw hexes push diffusion models toward synthetic flat digital fills. Describe real artist pigments harmonized to the researched brand palette (e.g., *unbleached warm cream cotton paper, raw sienna and warm ochre washes, deep walnut-sepia ink, soft olive chalk green, single touch of warm vermilion pencil*).

## 2. Concise `75-125` Word Natural Illustration Prompt Patterns

### A. Sunlit Editorial Ink-and-Watercolor Classroom / Human Story (`16:9`)
```text
Warm hand-drawn editorial ink-and-watercolor illustration on textured unbleached cream cotton paper, bright sunlit morning classroom in Vietnam filled with open natural window light and lively warmth.
In the foreground, a teacher in a pale oat linen shirt kneels at eye level beside a wooden desk, actively sketching a geometry arc in the margin of a student's open notebook while the teenager leans forward resting her chin on one hand, smiling with sudden understanding; a wooden ruler, eraser, and stacked textbooks clutter the desk naturally.
Softly sketched classmates work near tall bright sunlit windows in the airy background.
Expressive variable-weight sepia fountain-pen linework with tapered strokes and loose translucent washes of warm ochre, pale sage, and walnut ink, leaving generous untouched cream paper breathing space.
Authentic handmade sketchbook warmth, zero stiff vector outlines, zero flat corporate clip-art, zero dark gloom, zero text or watermarks.
```

### B. Expressive Editorial Spot Illustration / Object & Gesture Vignette (`4:3` or `16:9`)
```text
Loose editorial ink-and-gouache illustration on warm cold-press cotton rag paper, bright airy natural daylight.
<Specific concrete hands-in-action moment: e.g., a teacher's hand holding a worn brass fountain pen mid-sketch circling a lesson idea on an open notebook while a warm matte-ivory & brushed-bronze companion hand gently steadies the paper corner and tucks a small cream index tab beside the drawing>.
Organic tapered walnut-ink nib lines with subtle dry-brush texture, delicate translucent washes of warm honey bronze and raw umber with slight hand-printed imperfect register, open cream paper margins on all sides.
Natural human drawing rhythm, zero glossy 3D plastic, zero mechanical CAD perfection, zero neon, zero text.
```

## 3. Pass 4 Naturalness Vision Audit (Instant Reject Triggers)

Inspect every rendered illustration at full resolution (`2048px`) before accepting:
- [ ] **Alive Linework:** Do strokes taper, breathe, and feel genuinely drawn by a human hand? (Reject uniform sterile vector outlines or stiff CAD geometry!)
- [ ] **Concrete Mid-Action:** Can a viewer immediately name the EXACT physical action happening (sketching, measuring, explaining, passing, building) rather than a frozen vague pose?
- [ ] **Airy Context-Right Light:** Are teaching/daytime scenes bright, warm, and hopeful with open shadows? (Reject underexposed dark/gloomy rooms!)
- [ ] **Paper & Pigment Honesty:** Do washes soak softly into visible paper grain with natural negative space? (Reject shiny digital airbrush gradients or flat paint-bucket fills!)
