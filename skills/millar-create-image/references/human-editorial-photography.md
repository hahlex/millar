# Human Editorial & Documentary Photography Playbook

Consult this playbook whenever generating or art-directing **real human photography** (portraits, classrooms, workshops, studios, field journalism, founder/team portraits, community scenes, color OR black-and-white editorial series).

**Core Rule:** Author **100% of photographic prompts in crisp, idiomatic English** using authentic documentary cinematography vocabulary. Keep only real-world in-scene text (chalkboard writing, notebook headers, shop signs) in the target locale language when genuinely needed. Never hardcode an unrelated brand's palette: derive wardrobe, wall tone, and color grading strictly from the researched Brand DNA.

---

## 1. Defeating "AI Wax Skin" & Generic Western Stock Clichés

AI image models default to plastic airbrushed faces, blinding white teeth, fake bokeh studio rim lights, and sterile American-stock offices unless explicitly steered toward real documentary photography:

- **Authentic Skin & Micro-Imperfection Language (Always Include):**
  - Specify: *"natural unretouched skin texture with fine pores, subtle daylight sheen, realistic stray hairs, creased cotton fabric, lived-in matte surfaces, zero airbrushing, zero beauty-filter smoothing, zero plastic HDR glow."*
- **Candid Human Presence (Anti-Stock-Smile Rule):**
  - Photograph subjects **absorbed in real action or quiet thought**: eyes down on an open notebook, leaning slightly toward a chalkboard explanation, hands steady on a wooden desk, mid-conversation listening attentively, or looking calmly toward a window.
  - NEVER prompt *"smiling happily at the camera"*, *"diverse team high-fiving"*, or *"pointing excitedly at a laptop"*, as those triggers immediately activate generic stock-photo faces.
  - When a direct-to-lens editorial portrait IS desired, specify: *"calm composed expression, relaxed mouth, quiet intelligent gaze, natural slight asymmetry, documentary magazine portrait."*
- **Anatomically Safe Hand & Gesture Framing:**
  - Prompt simple, grounded hand actions: *"hands resting naturally on wooden desk beside an open notebook"*, *"right hand holding a piece of white chalk writing calmly"*, *"hands folded loosely in lap"*, or compose waist-up / shoulder-up portraits where fingers are not splayed toward the camera.
  - Avoid complex intertwined hands, crowded raised-hand clusters, or foreshortened fingers pointing at the lens (the #1 source of 6-finger AI artifacts).

---

## 2. Cultural, Geographic & Environmental Authenticity (Research First!)

Never drop people into generic Western open-plan glass offices or cliché tourist costumes. During `millar-deep-research`, ground the environment in the **actual place and everyday reality** of the brand's audience:

- **Vietnamese & Regional Authenticity Example (When the Brief Is Set in Vietnam):**
  - *Classrooms & Schools:* Natural daylight filtering through tall louvered timber/steel windows or open breezeway corridors, lime-washed cream/ochre plaster walls with subtle patina, seasoned wooden desks with worn brass inkwells or stacked paper notebooks, matte dark-green or slate chalkboard with subtle chalk dust, everyday modest teacher/student attire (simple collared shirts, ao dai only where culturally natural like a real school morning, ordinary schoolbags), with zero futuristic sci-fi holograms or US high-school lockers.
  - *Highland vs. Urban Contrast:* Authentic misty tiled roofs, timber verandas, red-earth hills, and overcast mountain daylight vs. humid urban balconies, concrete apartment louvers, and warm late-afternoon city street light, observed with dignity and calm documentary respect, never exoticized or caricatured.
- **Other Regions & Domains:** Apply the exact same research rigor: check real local architecture, window light geometry, furniture materials, climate, and everyday workwear before writing Block 2 of the prompt.

## 3. Cross-Shot-Scale Film Roll Continuity (One Photographer, One Afternoon)

Multi-page sites break visual unity when a wide classroom photo looks like cool smartphone HDR, the close-up portrait looks like a warm studio ad, and a B&W photo looks crushed and over-sharpened. Lock these **5 Camera-Roll Invariants** inside your 80% Fixed English Master Style Prefix:

| Continuity Axis | How to Lock Across Wide Group, Medium Moment, Tight Portrait & B&W |
|---|---|
| **1. Lens Family & Optical Character** | Stay inside one optical era (e.g., *"35mm documentary rangefinder look, gentle corner falloff, natural spherical bokeh"*) varying ONLY focal length cleanly (`28mm-35mm` wide scene -> `50mm` medium human interaction -> `85mm` tight intimate portrait). |
| **2. Shared Natural Light Direction & Quality** | Lock ONE light character across the whole roll (e.g., *"soft diffused overcast daylight entering from side windows at 40 degrees, gentle open shadows, warm shadow transition, zero artificial strobe flash"*). |
| **3. Shared Color Science & Film Grain** | Name one consistent film/color behavior grounded in the researched Brand DNA (e.g., *"muted natural film color science, slightly warm parchment highlights, soft matte shadow roll-off, fine organic 400-ISO film grain, desaturated natural greens and earth tones"*). |
| **4. Wardrobe & Environmental Palette Harmony** | Derive clothing and room materials from the researched brand's color temperature (e.g., muted linen, cream cotton, indigo denim, warm walnut, slate) reserving the brand's high-tension accent for ONE subtle organic prop (a notebook ribbon, brick wall glimpse, red ink mark) rather than neon clothing. |
| **5. Color-to-Monochrome B&W Continuity** | When mixing color photos with black-and-white documentary plates on secondary pages, keep the **identical lens, daylight direction, shadow roll-off, and fine film grain**, switching ONLY color science to: *"timeless silver-gelatin monochrome photography, rich midtone separation, soft highlight roll-off, luminous open shadows, matching fine 400-ISO film grain, zero harsh crushed blacks."* |

## 4. Concise 150-250 Word English Prompt Templates (Never Over-Specify!)

Keep prompts tight and alive (150-250 words). Never micromanage every background object.

### A. Wide Environmental / Classroom / Community Scene (`16:9` or `3:2`)
```text
Candid documentary editorial photography shot on 35mm lens, f/2.8, natural shallow depth of field,
[SCENE: researched authentic room/corridor/studio with real people absorbed in quiet work or dialogue, natural unposed posture, hands resting cleanly on desks or notebooks, calm uncluttered negative space on the <left/right> third for page typography],
soft diffused natural daylight streaming diagonally from tall side windows illuminating subtle dust in the air and casting soft architectural shadows across three depth planes,
natural unretouched skin textures, realistic lived-in garments and timber/plaster surfaces harmonized with <researched brand color language>,
muted matte film color science, organic fine 400-ISO grain, gentle shadow roll-off, quiet human dignity,
zero posed camera smiles, zero wax-smooth AI skin, zero artificial studio rim light, zero stock-photo gloss, zero overlaid text or watermarks.
```

### B. Intimate Tight Editorial Portrait (`4:3` or `3:4`)
```text
Intimate documentary editorial portrait shot on 85mm lens, f/2.2, crisp focus on the eyes with soft natural background falloff,
[SUBJECT: authentic person matching researched audience/region, head and shoulders framing, calm composed natural gaze slightly off-camera or relaxed toward lens, authentic age/skin pores/stray hairs, understated everyday clothing in <researched brand neutral/material palette>],
[IDENTICAL LOCKED LIGHTING, FILM SCIENCE, GRAIN & EXCLUSION HYGIENE FROM WIDE SHOT].
```

### C. Matching Black-and-White Documentary Plate (`3:2` or `4:3`)
```text
Timeless silver-gelatin black-and-white documentary photography shot on 50mm lens, f/2.4, matching the exact candid lens character and daylight angle of the color series,
[SUBJECT: quiet decisive human detail, e.g., chalk-dusted hand writing on a matte board or student leaning over an open sunlit book by a louvered window],
rich luminous midtones, open detailed shadows, subtle highlight bloom, authentic fine 400-ISO silver film grain, generous negative space,
zero harsh HDR contrast, zero airbrushed skin, zero posed stock expressions, zero watermarks.
```

## 5. Human-Photo Pass 4 Vision Audit (Instant Reject Checklist)

Open every generated human photo at full resolution before accepting it:
- [ ] **Skin & Eyes:** Real texture and natural catchlights (reject plastic/wax skin or dead glassy eyes).
- [ ] **Hands & Anatomy:** Exact 5 natural fingers, anatomically plausible wrists/shoulders, clean props (reject warped pens, melted chalk, or extra digits).
- [ ] **Expression Authenticity:** Feels like a real candid documentary frame, NOT a stock-photo commercial smile.
- [ ] **Cultural & Environmental Truth:** Architecture, daylight, furniture, and attire match the researched real-world context (zero accidental Western office/locker leaks).
- [ ] **Cross-Page Film Continuity:** Side-by-side with the Anchor Asset, all wide/medium/portrait/B&W shots look captured on the same camera roll in the same light.
- [ ] **Provenance Saved:** Exact English prompt recorded in `<filename>.prompt.json`.
