# Multi-Page Visual Asset Mapping Blueprint

Prevent secondary pages from falling into bare "industrial wireframe" mode by planning a cohesive multi-scale visual series across the entire site before coding.

## Scale Variation Matrix (Freeze Style, Vary Scale)

| Page / Route | Section Role | Recommended Subject Scale | Aspect Ratio | Framing & Integration Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Home (`/`)** | Hero Stage | Wide establishing scene or flagship stylized product showcase | `16:9` or `3:2` | Reserve clean negative space for H1 + CTA; never obscure headline contrast |
| **Features (`/features`)** | Capability Deep-Dives | Medium architectural objects, bespoke diagram plates, or interactive UI chrome | `4:3` or `16:10` | Alternate asymmetric Editorial Split and Spatial Stage; zero identical 3-card icon spam |
| **How It Works / Architecture** | Process & System Flow | Code-native SVG schematic (`ClosedLoopSchematic`, `LayerStackDiagram`) or precision macro geometry | `16:9` | Crisp 1px hairlines, tabular step numbering, zero text-stroke overlap |
| **Pricing (`/pricing`)** | Trust & Commitment | Quiet atmospheric horizon band or subtle tactile material plate | `21:9` / `16:9` | Ultra-low visual noise so pricing comparison table stays effortless to scan |
| **About / Craft (`/about`)** | Editorial Narrative | Human-scale studio environment, candid tactile details, or *Measured Warmth* poster plate | `3:2` or `4:5` | Pair with `65ch` editorial essay split and hairline pull-quote |
| **Blog / Docs (`/docs`)** | Reading Companion | Minimalist geometric chapter header plate (`4:1` or `16:9`) + inline clean diagrams | `16:9` | Restrained `Read` mode: imagery illuminates architecture rather than distracting from prose |
| **Product App (`/app`)** | Empty States & Onboarding | Crisp 1.5px icon mark or single restrained vector spot plate (`max 160px`), zero CSS gradients | `1:1` | Calm `Operate` mode: crisp solid surfaces, clear recovery action |

## Dedicated Single-Subject Catalog Plate Rule (Track M Anti-Crop Guard)

- **NEVER crop a multi-object hero or collection composite image into individual product/SKU/feature cards.** Cropping leaves sliced neighboring objects on card edges and shows wrong silhouettes in Quick-View drawers.
- Every distinct catalog item, SKU, or capability card that displays a visual MUST receive its own **dedicated single-subject plate** sharing the exact series lighting, margin, and background polarity.

## Visual Quality Self-Check Before Committing Any Image

1. Put Hero image and Secondary-page images side by side: do they look like they belong to the same exhibition/publication?
2. Does color temperature match the page background without harsh white-box or mismatched tint edges?
3. Are there zero garbled AI letters, warped hands, duplicated geometry, or unrequested watermarks?
4. Does the crop still frame the focal subject cleanly at `375px` mobile width?
